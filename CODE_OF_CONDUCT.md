# Code of conduct

Discuss changes with evidence and respect. Participation is open regardless of
experience, language, origin, age, gender or disability. Harassment, threats,
discriminatory remarks, sexual harassment and publishing another person's
private information without consent are not acceptable.

This policy applies to issues, pull requests and other spaces of this
repository. The maintainer may remove harmful content, close discussions or
restrict participation, depending on severity and repetition.

To report a concern, open an issue that asks the maintainer for a private
channel, without including the details or any private evidence. There is no
dedicated confidential reporting address yet.
